our thesis

The only data you can promise is private is the data that was never sent.

Every request your team sends to an AI tool carries more than they meant to send: a client's name, a live key, an account number.

The usual fix is to watch the wire, a cloud proxy that reads your traffic in order to protect it. But that just makes one more place your data lives. It doesn't remove the exposure. It relocates it.

Olo's premise is narrow and absolute. No our-cloud step, no copy to inspect. We redact on the device, before the request ever leaves it. Nothing sensitive goes out.
