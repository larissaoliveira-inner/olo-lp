[ 03 ] the design

Not a people problem. A design problem — with no our-cloud step.

1

Detect

A 14.7MB classifier reads the payload on the device, where it already lives.

2

Classify

Every sensitive span is identified on the device in about 4.5 milliseconds. That is on-device time, not end-to-end request latency.

3

Redact

Each span is rewritten in place. Nothing downstream to log, subpoena, or breach.
